Marking scheme

Mark strictly against these answers. Award a mark only where the required working or point is present. For maths and physics, a correct answer with no working scores nothing.

Q1 — model answer

$r=6$. $V=\tfrac43\pi(6)^3=\tfrac43\pi(216)=288\pi\approx904.8\text{ cm}^3$

Q2 — model answer

$P=IV=4.0 imes230=920\ ext{W}$.

Since 920 W is greater than 720 W, a 13 A fuse is suitable.

Power = 920 W; 13 A fuse.

Q3 — model answer

$E_h=cm\Delta T=902 imes0.50 imes40=18\,040\ ext{J}$.

Heat energy = $1.8 imes10^4$ J (2 s.f.).

Q4 — model answer

Ordered: 2,3,4,5,7,8,9,11. Median $=\dfrac{5+7}{2}=6$. Q1$=\dfrac{3+4}{2}=3.5$, Q3$=\dfrac{8+9}{2}=8.5$. IQR$=5$.

Q5 — model answer

Profits are usually negative or low. This is because heavy spending on promotion is needed to make customers aware of the product, but sales volumes are still small.

Q6 — model answer

Social media is far cheaper and lets a business target a specific group by age and interest (1). It can also reach young customers who watch less television, so the money is used more effectively (2).

Q7 — model answer

Favourable: 5, 6 → $\dfrac26=\dfrac13$

Q8 — model answer

$E_h=ml=0.10 imes2.26 imes10^6=2.26 imes10^5\ ext{J}$.

Heat energy = $2.3 imes10^5$ J (2 s.f.).

Q9 — model answer

$T_1=20+273=293\ ext{K}$; $T_2=40+273=313\ ext{K}$.

$p_2=\dfrac{p_1T_2}{T_1}=\dfrac{120 imes313}{293}=128.2\ ext{kPa}$.

New pressure = $1.3 imes10^2$ kPa (2 s.f.).

Q10 — model answer

Fat: eat less saturated fat and choose unsaturated oils — e.g. grill rather than deep fry and use olive/rapeseed oil. Fibre: eat more high-fibre/starchy carbohydrate — e.g. switch to wholemeal bread and brown rice. Salt: eat less salt — e.g. flavour food with herbs and spices instead of adding salt and check labels for hidden salt.

Q11 — model answer

Baking powder — raising agent (makes the mixture rise by releasing carbon dioxide). Cornflour — thickening agent (thickens liquid, used in sauces and clear glazes). Gelatine — setting agent (sets a liquid into a jelly/solid). Vanilla essence — flavouring (adds or improves flavour).

Q13 — model answer

Q14 — model answer

Q15 — model answer

Q16 — model answer

Q17 — model answer

Lower transport costs and a faster, shorter delivery time; also better for the local community and the environment.

Q18 — model answer

Ferrous metals contain iron and are magnetic but rust unless protected; non-ferrous metals contain no iron and resist rust.

Q19 — model answer

Q20 — model answer
