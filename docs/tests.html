📝 Practice exams

Every Sunday is a test day. Sit the paper in 55 minutes with no notes, then mark it in the second block and fill in the score tracker.

Why Sunday? Testing yourself is far more effective than re-reading. The score tracker shows exactly which topics to put back into next week.

How to mark your own paper

  • Mark strictly — an inflated score hides the topics you need to fix.
  • A correct answer with no working scores zero in maths and physics.
  • Anything under 70% goes back on next week's revision list.
