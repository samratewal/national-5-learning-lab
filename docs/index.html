How to use this site

  1. Open the plan. It assigns two 55-minute blocks to every night for 45 nights.
  2. Follow the links. Each block links straight to the topic page you need.
  3. Read, cover, redo. Read the rule, cover the worked example and reproduce it, then read the exam trap.
  4. Do the practice. Answers are hidden behind a toggle so you cannot accidentally read them.
  5. Mark past papers yourself. Use the official SQA marking instructions — that is the only reliable way to know what an A answer looks like.

What an A actually requires

Grade boundaries move every year, but at National 5 an A is roughly 70–75% of the total marks. That means you cannot afford to drop whole topics. The plan is built so that nothing is left out — the aim is coverage first, then speed and accuracy through past papers.

  • Maths: never write an answer without working — a correct answer with no working scores zero.
  • Physics: always state the relationship, substitute, then give the answer with units.
  • English: RUAE marks come from naming the technique and explaining its effect.
  • Business & Design: develop every point — one-word answers score nothing.
  • PE: link every factor to its impact on performance.
  • Cookery: safety and hygiene marks are free marks — never skip them.

Built from the official SQA National 5 course specifications. Past papers and marking instructions are free at sqa.org.uk/pastpapers.
