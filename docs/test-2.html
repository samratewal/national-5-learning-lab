Marking scheme

Mark strictly against these answers. Award a mark only where the required working or point is present. For maths and physics, a correct answer with no working scores nothing.

Q1 — model answer

Add: $5x=15$, so $x=3$. Then $6+y=11$, so $y=5$. Solution $(3,5)$.

Q2 — model answer

$m=\dfrac{11-2}{4-1}=\dfrac{9}{3}=3$

Q3 — model answer

'Oozed' (1) means to flow slowly and unpleasantly, like pus. This suggests his insincerity was excessive and almost physically unpleasant, making the reader distrust him (1).

Q4 — model answer

It has made journeys quicker / shortened travel time (1). It has damaged or built on open countryside (1). No words lifted.

Q5 — model answer

$y=2x+1 \Rightarrow x=\dfrac{y-1}{2}$, so $f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{x-1}{2}$

Q6 — model answer

$ac=-6$; numbers multiplying to $-6$ adding to $5$: $6,-1$. Split: $2x^2+6x-x-3=2x(x+3)-1(x+3)=(2x-1)(x+3)$. So $x=\tfrac12$ or $x=-3$.

Q7 — model answer

Fishing boat = primary; car factory = secondary; hotel = tertiary.

Q8 — model answer

Shareholders own part of the company and vote on major decisions at general meetings (1). If they are unhappy with performance they can vote out directors or sell their shares, which can lower the share price (2).

Q9 — model answer

$9^2+12^2=81+144=225$ and $15^2=225$. They are equal, so by the converse of Pythagoras the triangle is right-angled (right angle opposite the 15 cm side).

Q10 — model answer

$W=mg=1500 imes3.7=5550\ ext{N}$.

Weight = 5550 N ($approx5.6 imes10^3$ N, 2 s.f.).

Q11 — model answer

$\dfrac{72}{360}\times\pi\times20 = 0.2\times\pi\times20 = 4\pi \approx 12.6$ cm

Q12 — model answer

The radii meet the tangents at 90°. In quadrilateral OAPB: $90+90+68+\theta=360$, so $\theta=112°$.

Q13 — model answer

Metaphor (1). Just as gnarled roots are twisted, tough and weathered (2), the comparison suggests the man's hands were rough and aged and that he has worked hard for a long time (3).

Q14 — model answer

'The seat height should be 450 mm ± 10 mm and the backrest should support the lumbar region when seated for 60 minutes.'

Q15 — model answer

Combine. For example, combining a torch and a pen into one product so the user carries fewer items.

Q16 — model answer

It lists the criteria the finished product must meet, giving designers a clear target and a basis for evaluating the final design.

Q17 — model answer

Interviewing people who cook regularly about grip and comfort; observing users preparing food to see where existing utensils cause difficulty.

Q18 — model answer

Changes in corporation tax affect profits; changes to import tariffs or trading rules (e.g. post-Brexit) affect the cost of goods brought in from abroad.

Q19 — model answer

All four Ps should signal the same quality message (1). A high price, quality product, exclusive place and prestigious promotion together create a premium image — discounting would damage it (2).

Q20 — model answer

Cut a small cross in the base of each tomato. Lower into boiling water for 15–30 seconds, then transfer straight to iced/cold water (refresh). The skin loosens and slips off easily. It is done to remove the skin cleanly without cooking the flesh, and to stop enzyme action that would discolour or soften the fruit.

Q21 — model answer

If the goalkeeper loses focus, they may react late to a shot or fail to track the ball (1), allowing a goal that a focused keeper would have saved (2).
