Marking scheme

Mark strictly against these answers. Award a mark only where the required working or point is present. For maths and physics, a correct answer with no working scores nothing.

Q1 — model answer

A job description lists the duties and responsibilities of the post. A person specification lists the qualifications, skills and qualities the ideal candidate should have.

Q2 — model answer

New staff learn health and safety rules, reducing accidents; they understand policies and settle in faster, reducing early mistakes.

Q3 — model answer

Job rotation moves staff between different tasks to add variety. Job enrichment gives staff more responsibility and autonomy within their role.

Q4 — model answer

Requisitioning is working out the exact ingredients, quantities and cost needed to make the required number of portions, and writing them as an ordered list. It is important so that the correct amount of food is bought, nothing is wasted or runs short, the meal is costed within budget, and shopping is quick and organised.

Q5 — model answer

1) Insert a clean skewer/toothpick into the centre — it should come out clean, with no raw mixture stuck to it. 2) Press the centre lightly — it should feel firm and springy and the cake should have shrunk slightly away from the sides of the tin.

Q6 — model answer

A bank overdraft — short term and flexible.

Q7 — model answer

Contribution $=15-6=\pounds9$. Break-even $=\dfrac{4500}{9}=500$ units.

Q8 — model answer

Sequence of operations; materials and quantities; tools and equipment; quality checks (also acceptable: time plan, health and safety notes).

Q9 — model answer

A jig holds the workpiece and guides the tool to the correct position. A fixture only holds the workpiece securely without guiding the tool.

Q10 — model answer

Use recycled or sustainably sourced materials; design the product to be durable and repairable so it lasts longer.

Q11 — model answer

The danger zone is 5 °C to 63 °C. Bacteria multiply fastest in this range, so food left in it can quickly become unsafe to eat. Food should be kept below 5 °C for cold storage or above 63 °C when being held hot, and cooled or reheated quickly through the danger zone.

Q12 — model answer

Short bursts of high-intensity work followed by rest periods (1). This trains the body to work anaerobically and recruit fast-twitch muscle fibres, increasing maximum pace (2).

Q13 — model answer

Video analysis of technique; a training diary recording sessions and results (also acceptable: observation schedule, fitness testing, heart rate monitoring).
