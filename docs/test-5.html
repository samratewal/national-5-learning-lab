Marking scheme

Mark strictly against these answers. Award a mark only where the required working or point is present. For maths and physics, a correct answer with no working scores nothing.

Q1 — model answer

$v=f\lambda=2000 imes0.15=300\ ext{m s}^{-1}$.

Wave speed = 300 m s$^{-1}$ (2 s.f.).

Q2 — model answer

$f=100\ ext{MHz}=1.0 imes10^8\ ext{Hz}$.

$\lambda=\dfrac{c}{f}=\dfrac{3.0 imes10^8}{1.0 imes10^8}=3.0\ ext{m}$.

Wavelength = 3.0 m (2 s.f.).

Q3 — model answer

His fingers tightened around the folder. The door handle felt cold and too smooth. He wiped his palm on his trousers and pushed it open.

Q4 — model answer

Raw materials; work in progress; finished goods.

Q5 — model answer

Flow/mass production.

Q6 — model answer

A word or phrase that signals the relationship between ideas, e.g. 'however', 'furthermore', 'consequently'. They guide the reader through your argument and improve structure marks.

Q7 — model answer

1) Use a separate (colour-coded) chopping board and knife for the raw chicken and keep the salad preparation completely apart. 2) Wash hands, the board and any equipment thoroughly with hot soapy water after handling the raw chicken and before touching the salad; also store the raw chicken covered on the bottom shelf of the fridge.

Q8 — model answer

Products must be recalled or replaced, which is expensive; customers lose trust and go to competitors, reducing future sales.

Q9 — model answer

Increased recruitment and training costs; loss of experienced staff and reduced service quality.

Q10 — model answer

A dovetail joint — the interlocking angled pins resist being pulled apart, making it very strong for drawer corners.

Q11 — model answer

To protect the wood from moisture and staining; to improve its appearance and enhance the grain.

Q12 — model answer

A coping saw.

Q13 — model answer

Clamp the workpiece and wear eye protection; ensure the chuck guard is in place before starting.

Q14 — model answer

Speed increases (from $2.3 imes10^8$ to $3.0 imes10^8$ m s$^{-1}$). Frequency is constant, so by $v=f\lambda$ the wavelength increases. The ray bends away from the normal (provided the angle of incidence is greater than 0°).

Speed increases; wavelength increases; ray bends away from the normal.

Q15 — model answer

Greater flexibility allows the gymnast to achieve a wider range of movement at the joints (1), so they can complete splits and stretched positions required in routines and reduce injury risk (2).
